# Act I: The Bleeding Veil (design, written by the game's author)

The border province of Oakhaven sits along an ancient fault line where the barrier separating the mortal realm from the Abyssal Core wears dangerously thin. Fed by the panic of a starving frontier populace, an oily, crimson-tinged corruption known as The Taint has begun seeping into root systems, subterranean aquifers, and consecrated burial sites. Where the local concentration of dread peaks, reality tears open into jagged Bleed Rifts, allowing grotesque entities to manifest.

Look: Act 1 is woodland only, with skeletons, zombies, souls, a sick village, caverns, caves and crypts.

## Main questline: The Scourge of Oakhaven

**Chapter 1: The First Incursion**
- Objective: clear the gate perimeter of Oakhaven Outpost and push into Blackroot Wilds to locate Scout Captain Dennis.
- Events: find Dennis pinned inside an abandoned hunting lodge surrounded by Tainted Briarwolves. Rescue him and retrieve a Coagulated Rift Shard dropped by the pack leader.
- Return: present the shard to High Priestess Althea in the Outpost, who reveals the shard is not natural poison but condensed demonic egress feeding on the hamlet's collective terror.

**Chapter 2: Poisoning the Well**
- Objective: enter The Weeping Hollow to find the subterranean tributary feeding Oakhaven's freshwater well.
- Events: navigate the limestone shafts to The Brood Cyst, a massive fleshy mass anchored to the riverbed. Slay the guardian, Skittering Matron Vhol, and plant an Alchemical Purge Ward to temporarily cleanse the water supply.
- Twist: destroying the cyst does not eradicate the Taint; the dying beast's blood tears a localized fissure that screams with demonic whispers pointing toward the ancestral tombs to the north.

**Chapter 3: Breaking the Seal**
- Objective: march through the Ashen Verge to reach The Grand Ossuary. Find the shattered halves of the Warded Keystone needed to unlock the heavy iron gates.
- Events: one half is held by a maddened grave warden who willingly drank the Taint; the other is lodged inside a mutated Golem animated by a Bleed Rift. Combine both at the sealed mausoleum entrance.

**Chapter 4: Descent into Madness**
- Objective: fight down through The Grand Ossuary (Levels 1 and 2) to prevent the total collapse of the local boundary between realms.
- Events: descend through suffocating catacombs while hunting down three Veil Anchors being channeled by cultist heralds. Shattering each anchor destabilizes the immediate floor and triggers elite pack ambushes.

**Chapter 5: Act boss, Lord Malaphax, The Flesh-Torn**
- Arena: The Sepulcher of Mourning.
- Concept: formerly Duke Edward of Oakhaven, who entombed himself alive to escape his fear of death, only for the Taint to warp his panic into an insatiable demonic engine.
- Phase 1 (The Burden of Iron): a towering corrupted knight with a cracked executioner's greatsword: sweeping cleaves and localized ground-tears that erupt with boiled blood.
- Phase 2 (The Rift Flesh), at 50% HP: he rips open his chest plate, exposing a pulsing mini-rift. He drops his sword to sprout bone scythes, gaining erratic dashes, ceiling-drop attacks, and dragging the player into a darkened "Veil Realm" overlay where shadows deal passive ticking damage until an ambient Rift Soul is destroyed.
- Resolution: Malaphax collapses into ash, but the rift behind the altar remains a jagged, weeping scar. Priestess Althea wards the doorway, warning that the rot in Act I was merely a symptom: the epicenter lies across the eastern mountain pass in the barren wastes of Act II.

## Side quests

- **The Lost Heirloom**: a fleeing merchant in town promises an uncut socket gem if you venture into a ruined manor in the Blackroot Wilds to recover his family lockbox. Opening it reveals a disturbing diary showing he knowingly sacrificed his servants to feed the corruption in exchange for safe passage.
- **Restless Ancestors**: a weeping spirit in The Grand Ossuary (Level 1) asks you to recover four desecrated burial urns scattered across the crypt sarcophagi and place them back on their sanctified pedestals, granting a permanent passive resistance bonus.
- **A Taste for Rot**: an apothecary cast out of town hides in a cave within the Sunken Fen. He needs three intact glands from mutated Fen Crawlers to brew a mutagenic draught. Completing it awards a choice of a rare attribute-enhancing elixir or a socketable skill rune.
- **The Last Watch**: track down three missing scouts throughout the Ashen Verge. Two are found horribly mutated and must be put down; the sole survivor gives a rusted key opening a locked armory vault hidden behind a collapsible wall in the Ossuary.

## Dynamic world encounters

These spawn randomly across the open woodland and cavern maps, rewarding crafting materials, rare equipment, or socket currencies.

| Event | Objective and mechanics |
|---|---|
| Cursed Shrine | Interact with a bleeding obelisk. Spawns 3 rapid waves of aggressive fiends within 45 seconds. Cleansing it yields an empowered 2-minute buff and a cluster of high-tier loot. |
| Tears in Reality | A volatile Bleed Rift rapidly expands on the map, pulling the player inward while vomiting out elite monsters. Destroy 3 mini-spires tethering the rift before the timer expires to seal it. |
| The Dying Pilgrim | Defend an injured paladin channeling a purification circle against waves of rot fiends. If the paladin survives with more than 50% HP, he opens a hidden consecrated reliquary chest. |
| Pyre of the Dead | Ignite a pile of contaminated corpses before they finish transforming. Lighting the pyre enrages the surrounding swarm: a sudden, dense horde brawl in a confined fire ring. |

## Spawning mini-dungeons (sub-caverns and cellars)

Non-mandatory cave entrances, trapdoors and stone grates scattered across exterior zones, each with a random chance to spawn per run:

- **Rot-Damp Cellar**: a single-room underground root cellar swarming with blood maggots and a guaranteed gold-hoarding mimic chest.
- **Forgotten Bone Pit**: a compact multi-room cavern littered with skeletal debris. Stepping into the center triggers an iron portcullis trap, locking you in with an elite dual-wielding skeleton champion.
- **Secluded Hermit's Sanctum**: a quiet cavern sanctuary with an abandoned crafting bench that allows a one-time free modification / socket reroll on any piece of gear.
- **Weeping Fissure**: a subterranean pocket where the realm wall is almost completely shredded. Extreme darkness and high enemy density, but a guaranteed Tainted Chest that requires killing all tethered elite guardians to unlock.
